## The Importance of Seasonal Roof Maintenance

Proactive seasonal upkeep protects your home's structural integrity and
prolongs the life of your entire roofing system. Prepping your roof before
extreme summer heat or winter ice storms ensures maximum safety, optimal attic
ventilation, and year-round protection.

  * **Significant Cost Savings:** Identifying minor seal gaps or damaged shingles early prevents sudden, catastrophic structural leaks.
  * **Improved Energy Efficiency:** Proper roof ventilation and dry attic insulation keep your home cooler in summer and warmer in winter, reducing energy consumption.
  * **Extended Roof Lifespan:** Routine cleaning and quick repairs minimize daily weather strain, adding years to your roof 's functional life.
  * **Enhanced Home Protection:** Regular inspections prevent hidden mold growth, deck rot, and foundation damage caused by overflowing water.

## Seasonal Roof Care & Inspection Guide

Keeping up with routine seasonal maintenance prevents minor wear from becoming
expensive water damage and extends the lifespan of your roof.

### **Spring: Post-Winter Care**

  * **Inspect Shingles:** Scan for missing, cracked, or curling shingles damaged by winter freezing and thawing cycles.
  * **Clear Drainage:** Remove leaves, sediment, and loose shingle granules from gutters and downspouts.
  * **Check Ground Level:** Use binoculars from the safety of the ground to safely inspect flashing around chimneys and roof vents.
  * **Examine the Attic:** Inspect the interior attic space for damp insulation or fresh water spots following heavy spring rainfall.

### **Summer: Heat & Growth Control**

  * **Assess Sun Damage:** Look for surface warping, heavy granule loss, or heat blistering caused by prolonged UV exposure.
  * **Trim Overhanging Trees:** Cut back tree branches hovering near or touching the roof line to prevent animal access and physical abrasion.
  * **Verify Ventilation:** Ensure soffit and ridge vents are clear so hot, humid air can escape the attic efficiently.
  * **Treat Algae & Moss:** Safely remove early moss or algae growth during dry weather using an approved roof cleaner to prevent moisture retention.

### **Fall: Pre-Winter Prep**

  * **Clear Fallen Debris:** Sweep leaves, pine needles, and twigs out of roof valleys and gutter tracks.
  * **Seal Flashing:** Inspect and reseal metal flashing around skylights, vents, and chimneys using exterior roofing sealant.
  * **Repair Damaged Shingles:** Apply asphalt roofing cement under loose shingle tabs or replace cracked shingles to withstand strong seasonal winds.
  * **Divert Water:** Ensure downspout extensions channel runoff at least 3–5 feet away from your home 's foundation.

### **Winter: Storm & Moisture Monitoring**

  * **Ground Checks:** Conduct quick visual inspections from the ground following severe winter storms to spot wind damage or ice dams.
  * **Watch Interior Surfaces:** Check top-floor ceilings and walls for new paint peeling, bubbling, or moisture staining.
  * **Monitor Flat Roofs:** Keep flat roof sections free from heavy standing water or ice blockages.
  * **Inspect Attic Space:** Periodically check the attic for winter condensation, frost buildup, or inadequate airflow.
